Severity reminder

Use the incident’s actual scope and impact when selecting a severity.

Severity 1: Critical Situation: System-wide outage affecting all users and business operations (e.g. all users affected by System/Tool down, website payment method down, website not accessible to customers).

If the issue is identified as Critical, immediately inform CCM.

Severity 2: High Situation: Major functionality down, impacting a significant portion of users (>5 hc affected / channel).
Severity 3: Medium Situation: Minor issue affecting a small group of users or non-critical functionality (<=5 hc affected / channel).

The reference does not define a Low severity threshold; follow your team’s escalation guidance for Low-severity incidents.

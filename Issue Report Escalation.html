Severity reminder

Use the incident’s actual scope and impact when selecting a severity.

Severity 1: CriticalSystem-wide outage affecting all users and business operations.

If critical, immediately inform CCM.

Severity 2: HighMajor functionality down, affecting more than 5 users/channels.
Severity 3: MediumMinor issue affecting 5 or fewer users/channels or non-critical functionality.

Follow your team’s escalation guidance for Low-severity incidents.

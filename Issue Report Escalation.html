Severity reminder

Use the incident’s actual scope and impact when selecting severity.

CriticalSevere business or customer impact, widespread outage, or urgent operational risk.

Escalate immediately.

HighMajor impact with prompt action required.
MediumModerate impact or a workaround is available.
LowLimited impact or low operational risk.

When in doubt, describe the impact clearly and use the least ambiguous severity option.
